Assumptions, limitations & data relationships
  • Energy ↔ Order linkage: Energy is allocated to orders by overlapping time windows of meter readings with order start/end. When windows are missing, energy is left unallocated (shown as “orphan” energy).
  • Productive vs idle energy: Approximate split uses stoppage minutes vs planned production time. True machine-state signals are not available → treat as estimate only.
  • MES vs Excel: Quantity variance = (MES − Excel) / Excel × 100. Negative = MES lower than Excel.
  • OEE: Simplified Availability × Performance × Quality. Quality assumed ~98 % when scrap data missing.
  • Staffing (MA): Headcount per order; no skill-level or role differentiation in data.
  • Density: From ERP; missing density flagged in quality checks. Groups are fixed bins for comparability.
  • Sample size: Current embedded data is illustrative. Real analysis must report n per finding and confidence.
  • Missing direct cost: Electricity price can be applied externally (e.g. 0.18 €/kWh) for financial impact of stoppages.
